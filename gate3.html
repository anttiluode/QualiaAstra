Can a world learn without labels?

Two interference sensors discover two states from their own raw waveforms, without source-sign labels. When B and C are actually observed during training, their future-minus-prediction errors shape the internal decoder. At test, only raw sensors and a clue at A remain.

Self-supervised development · no labels or sign teachers
01 / Calibration

Learn from consequences

256 unlabelled episodes form two clusters per interference sensor. Each delayed B/C waveform then trains its own prediction through a local error. The test worlds are separate.

03 / Causality

Change the message

During learning the later B/C recordings provide prediction errors; during testing these recordings are strictly withheld. A cut or reversal acts only on an internal message.
