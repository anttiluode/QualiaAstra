Every result on this page concerns an engineered three-event Bayesian chain, not a spiking neuron or subjective experience. The distinction between a predicted event and fresh evidence is a mathematical requirement once correlated messages can be replayed. Whether a real dendritic circuit learns trustworthy provenance is a separate unsolved task. Frozen protocol and limitations.
