Can the sensors teach the world?

Two interference sensors record anonymous waveforms, not ready-made AB and BC relations. Separate labelled calibration teaches a small observation model. Later, one clue at A must revise predictions of entirely withheld B and C recordings.

Development experiment · not a neural or consciousness result
01 / Calibration

Learn from raw traces

256 fresh, labelled calibration trials fit both interference sensors and the withheld-sensor response templates. The test cases are generated independently.

Change the message
