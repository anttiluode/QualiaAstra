# Shared prediction as a candidate structure of experience

Antti's hypothesis: local messages jointly sustain an internal possible world;
the role of a message is revealed by changes in predicted sensory consequences
when that message is intervened on. This offers an operational account of causal
predictive role. It is not a test for the presence of subjective experience.

Three distinct objects: incoming sensory consequences, internally predicted
consequences, and their residual. A small residual need not mean an empty
prediction. Conversely, a zero posterior mean can hide two incompatible possible
worlds; show the alternatives and uncertainty, not only the mean.

## Approaches considered

* Attribute existing damping timers: close to StateShapedPingRecall; useful for
  explaining a waveform, but a fixed additive decomposition cannot establish
  joint interpretation. Leave the independent Claude version free to pursue it.
* Add another adaptive wave rule: preserves the substrate, but combines timing,
  inference and credit assignment before the target capability is isolated.
* **Chosen here:** small learned generative model with distributed inference,
  deliberate ambiguity and causal message controls. An interpretable reference
  mechanism for later transfer into the wave body. No wave-substrate claim.

## Proposed bridge back to the family

RoundAndRound contributes the conceptual precedent of multiple learned response
operators and evidence-driven re-entry. StateShapedPingRecall contributes the
separation of a generated expectation from the sensory residual and the warning
that recall is not automatically hidden-cause inference. MusicalDelayLabyrinth
and TATWATASW are timing/order precedents, not imported engines. No source code
or scientific result is inherited from those repositories.

The measured question is whether a local clue revises remote predictions through
relationships, including correct uncertainty before disambiguation. The harder
next gate is learning those relationships and variables without their identities
being supplied, then testing new source combinations or interventions. None of
these gates establishes phenomenal experience or chooses among consciousness
theories. "Shared" is used here for coordinated inference, not as a synonym for
global workspace or integrated information.
